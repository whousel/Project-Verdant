# **Project-Verdant  — Requirements**



### **Version 1 — Completed**



##### **Monitoring**

* Continuously monitor soil moisture.
* Monitor the water level of the reservoir.
* Detect when the reservoir is empty or below the required water level.
* Provide a visible LED indicator for the data-logging system.



##### **Automatic Watering**

* Automatically activate the pump when soil moisture falls below the configured threshold.
* Automatically stop watering when the desired moisture level is reached.
* Deliver water through a distributed watering system designed to provide more consistent coverage.
* Keep water contained within the planter and reservoir during operation. 



##### **Data Logging**

* Record soil moisture readings over time.
* Record water-level status.
* Record pump state.
* Store testing data on a microSD card for later analysis.



##### **Reliability and Safety**

* Operate continuously without requiring manual intervention during normal operation.
* Protect the electronics from accidental water exposure.
* Prevent water from escaping the intended watering area.
* Maintain stable operation during extended testing.
* Use a splash shield as an additional layer of protection for the electronics.



##### **Physical Design**

* Use custom 3D-printed components to secure and route system components.
* Secure the pump and watering tubing.
* Provide a compact enclosure for the primary electronics.
* Use removable containers for the reservoir and growing medium.



### **Future Development — Planned**



##### **Plant-Specific Control**

* Add predefined plant profiles with different recommended moisture conditions.
* Allow users to select and modify plant profiles.
* Allow watering thresholds and other settings to be changed directly on the device or through an application.



##### **Environmental Monitoring**

* Add temperature monitoring.
* Add airflow monitoring.
* Use environmental data to improve watering decisions and plant care.



##### **Improved Watering**

* Redesign the watering system for more consistent water distribution.
* Improve pressure regulation and water flow.
* Improve drainage and water routing.
* Make the system easier to refill.



##### **User Experience**

* Develop a more refined external enclosure.
* Improve the LED indicator system and integrate it into the enclosure.
* Improve the overall appearance and organization of the system.
* Make maintenance, refilling, and component access easier.



##### **Safety and Reliability**

* Improve protection against water exposure and electrical damage.
* Improve long-term unattended operation.
* Continue testing toward a system that can safely operate for extended periods without supervision.
* Improve the reliability of mechanical and electrical connections.



##### **Expanded Applications**

* Support nutrient-enriched water when appropriate for the selected growing method.
* Explore adaptation for hydroponic and other automated growing applications.
